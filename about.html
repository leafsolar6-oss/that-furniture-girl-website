Our story

Made by hand, in Ibadan.

You found us on TikTok — probably while scrolling past a cream boucle sectional you couldn't stop thinking about. Behind every one of those videos is a workshop in Iyaganku where the frames are cut, foamed and upholstered by hand.

How it started

From a phone camera to a full workshop.

It started with videos. Short clips of sofas being finished, fabrics being chosen, deliveries going out — posted from Ibadan to anyone who would watch. People started messaging. "Do you deliver to Lagos?" "Can you make this in brown?" "How much for the L-shape?"

So we built more. Today That Furniture Girl is a handcrafted furniture business with a showroom and workshop at Iyaganku, Ibadan, delivering to homes and offices across Nigeria. Same-day delivery is standard in Ibadan, most pieces are built in 5–10 working days, and nothing leaves the workshop until it's right.

Our customers aren't strangers — they found us, watched the process, and stayed. That's a responsibility we take seriously. Every piece is built to last, and every price is confirmed before we start cutting.

The craft

What goes into one piece of furniture

  • Frames — treated wood, joined and reinforced for heavy daily use
  • Foam — high-density fill that keeps its shape instead of sagging
  • Fabric — boucle, corduroy, floral and leather, with options sent to your phone
  • Carcass work — wardrobes, tables and TV stands in HDF and MDF
  • Finishing — hand-stitched details, tufting, piping and custom colours
