Quick answers

Before you message

We're always active — but here are the questions that come up most.

Do you deliver outside Ibadan?

Yes. Same-day delivery is available across Ibadan, and we arrange delivery to other states nationwide. Send us your address and we'll tell you the cost before you commit.

How soon can I get my furniture?

Some pieces are available for immediate pickup from the showroom. Built-to-order pieces usually take 5–10 working days — wardrobes around 5 days, centre tables around 10 — and large bespoke commissions take longer.

Can I pay on delivery?

Payment arrangements are agreed on WhatsApp when you order, and we'll always spell out what is due and when before we start building. Ask us directly and we'll explain the options for your piece.

I'm not in Nigeria — can I still order?

Absolutely. We build for customers abroad who are furnishing a home, an apartment or a family property in Nigeria. We'll send photos and videos of progress so you can approve finishes from wherever you are.
