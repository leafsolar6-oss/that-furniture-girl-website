Touch before you choose — fabrics & boards 8

Bouclé

Cloud-soft loops under the palm — warm to sit on, dry to the touch, and the most forgiving fabric we weave with.

Wear rating 3/5

Great for family living: the nubby weave hides creases and small marks. Keep claws away — loops can snag if they get pulled.

See the 16 pieces built with it →

Velvet

A dense pile that shifts from light to deep as it catches the room — smooth, quietly glamorous, noticeably cool at first touch.

Wear rating 4/5

Handles daily sitting well because the pile is dense; light crushing lifts back with a pass of steam. Rotate cushions to keep the nap even.

See the 11 pieces built with it →

Corduroy

Raised ridges (wales) with a dry, tactile grip — casual warmth that still reads grown-up on a tailored frame.

Wear rating 3/5

The ridges soften and gently smooth over the busiest seat through the years, but the colour stays honest and it re-fluffs easily.

See the 5 pieces built with it →

Leather & leather-cloth

Cool and glass-smooth at first touch, warming fast under you, with a light pad of foam between hand and frame.

Wear rating 5/5

The toughest face we put on a frame: wipes clean in a pass, drinks no spills, and patinas rather than wears out.

See the 15 pieces built with it →

Dry matte fabric

A tight plain weave with zero shine — quiet, even colour that never slips under you or reflects the lights away.

Wear rating 3/5

An honest everyday cloth: the matte face keeps colours flat and true, and the weave takes spot-cleaning without shading.

See the 8 pieces built with it →

Natural wood

Solid treated timber, sanded and sealed so the grain still runs under your fingers — the part of the piece that carries everything.

Wear rating 5/5

Frames, bed rails and legs are built to outlive the fabric on them. A coat of polish revives it; just don’t leave standing water on top.

See the 16 pieces built with it →

HDF board

High-density fibreboard milled glass-smooth and painted in satin — the surface stays flawlessly flat and the edges stay razor-sharp.

Wear rating 4/5

Our workhorse for wardrobe doors, consoles and shutters: dense enough to shrug off knocks — just wipe spills rather than leaving them.

See the 12 pieces built with it →

MDF board

Medium-density fibre: perfectly uniform, dead-flat and stable — the right base when the finish is a flawless lacquer or gloss top.

Wear rating 3/5

Stable and smooth for years in the living room; seal cut edges in wet areas and keep it off permanently damp floors.

See the 8 pieces built with it →

Questions

Before you ask

How do I order?+

On WhatsApp only. Tap Chat on any piece and a pre-filled message opens with the piece name and price — send it and we take it from there.

How long does a build take?+

It depends on the piece. Some are available for immediate pickup; most builds quote between 5 to 14 working days or more depending on the design; ornate sets like the Royal Kinging takes about three months.

Can I change the colour, size or fabric?+

Yes. Every piece is built to order, so colour, size and design follow your specification — send a reference and we quote it.

Do you deliver outside Ibadan?+

Yes — same-day delivery inside Ibadan and nationwide shipping across Nigeria. Delivery is free on orders over ₦2,000,000 (T&Cs apply).

Can I visit before ordering?+

Yes. The showroom is at Iyaganku, New GRA, Ibadan — message us on WhatsApp and we will plan a time for you to come and sit on the pieces.
