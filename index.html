FAQs

Questions? Look here.

Can't find your answer?

We are almost always active on WhatsApp. Send a message with what you are looking for and we will come back to you straight away — photos, prices and timelines included.

Ask a question
How do I place an order?

Message us on WhatsApp at 0907 984 9626 with the piece you want, or send a DM on Instagram (@thatfurnituregirl__) or TikTok (@thatfurnituregirl_). Tell us your colour and size preference and we'll confirm availability, price and delivery.

How long does production take?

Most pieces take 5–10 working days. Wardrobes are usually about 5 days, centre tables about 10 days, and some pieces are available for immediate pickup from the showroom. Large bespoke commissions like the Royal Kinging Set take about 3 months.

Can I change the colour, size or design?

Yes — that is what we do. Every piece can be made in the colour of your choice, resized to fit your room, or redesigned. Send us a photo of what you have in mind.

Do you deliver, and how much does it cost?

We offer same-day delivery in Ibadan and our delivery is quick. Delivery to other states nationwide can be arranged — the cost depends on where you are, so message us for a quote.

Can I see the furniture in person first?

Of course. Our showroom is at Iyaganku, New GRA, Ibadan, and walk-ins are welcome. Message us a little ahead of your visit and we'll have the pieces you're interested in ready for you.

Do you make pieces that aren't on the website?

We build beds, dining sets, office furniture, TV stands and complete house packages. If you can picture it, send us a photo or a sketch and we'll tell you what it costs to build.
