Questions

Before you ask

How do I order?+

On WhatsApp only. Tap Chat on any piece and a pre-filled message opens with the piece name and price — send it and we take it from there.

How long does a build take?+

It depends on the piece. Some are available for immediate pickup; most builds quote between 5 and 14 working days; ornate sets like the Royal Kinging take about three months.

Can I change the colour, size or fabric?+

Yes. Every piece is built to order, so colour, size and design follow your specification — send a reference and we quote it.

Do you deliver outside Ibadan?+

Yes — same-day delivery inside Ibadan and nationwide shipping across Nigeria. Delivery is free on orders over ₦2,000,000 (T&Cs apply).

Can I visit before ordering?+

Yes. The showroom is at Iyaganku, New GRA, Ibadan — message us on WhatsApp and we will plan a time for you to come and sit on the pieces.
