Before you order

Straight answers

Anything else you'd like to know, just ask — we're always active on WhatsApp.

Are the prices on this page final?

They are the prices for the pieces exactly as photographed and described. If you change the fabric, colour or size, or ask for extra detailing, the price may change — we'll always confirm the new price before we start building.

What if the piece I want is out of stock?

Almost nothing is kept "in stock" — most pieces are built to order, so there is no waiting for a shipment. We'll tell you the production time and keep you updated with photos as it comes together.

How do I pay?

Payment details are shared when you place your order on WhatsApp. Ask us about options for made-to-order pieces before we begin production.

Can I mix and match pieces?

Yes. Many customers order a sectional plus a centre table, or a whole living room of matching pieces. Tell us the room and we'll help you put a set together.

Found the one?

Send us the piece name and we'll confirm availability, price and delivery straight away.
